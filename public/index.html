    Calibration

    Measure from your eyes to the screen with a ruler, sit still, press Calibrate distance. That fixes the webcam FOV. Then use "Eye height nudge" until the box's floor looks level.

    Holo Modeler

    Say what you want and Claude Fable models it in a Blender that runs hidden in the background. The model appears in a 3D box behind your screen: the webcam tracks your eyes so the box looks deep, and your hands reach in and hold it.

    1. Use Microsoft Edge: its speech recognition is what hears you. Enter your screen size, press Start with webcam, then allow the camera and microphone.
    2. Press F for fullscreen (the 3D is only correct full screen). Sit about 50 cm away.
    3. Say "make a victorian teapot", or press / and type it. Then point at a part and say "make that gold". Every build is saved: "go back a version", "go back to version 2", "save version", "export".
    4. Point at the model to highlight a part; pinch to grab the model and push toward the screen to move it deeper. Pinch with both hands to turn and resize it.
    5. Pointing at a part: "focus on that", "hide that", "show only that", "show all", "quick red" / "quick metal" (instant, no Fable). "Clay view" shows the bare form. Sculpting comes in the next build.

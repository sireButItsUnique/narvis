Calibration

Measure from your eyes to the screen with a ruler, sit still, press Calibrate distance. That fixes the webcam FOV. Then use "Eye height nudge" until the box's floor looks level.

Head-Tracked Window

Your screen becomes a window into a 3D box. The webcam tracks your eyes and redraws the scene from where you are.

  1. Enter your screen size, then press Start with webcam and allow the camera.
  2. Press F for fullscreen (the 3D is only correct full screen). Sit about 50 cm away.
  3. Move your head side to side. Close one eye for the strongest effect.
  4. Raise a hand in front of the screen: point at an object, pinch to grab, push toward the screen to move it deeper.
